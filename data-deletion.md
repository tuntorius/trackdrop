## TrackDrop Data Deletion Policy

TrackDrop does not collect personal information or maintain user accounts. If you have paired the app with the PC browser extension and wish to delete your data, you can do so at any time by:

1. Clicking the "Unpair Device" button in the browser extension.

2. Uninstalling the TrackDrop app from your device, which clears all local cache and tokens.

If you have any questions or wish to manually request data deletion, please contact us at: tuntori-dev@gmail.com
